Product Description

This product contains a mixture of organic carbon, which increases seed germination and white roots. 15 days after seed germination, when the plant comes out of the ground, using this product increases the branches and the leaves become thicker.

The height of the plants increases and the number of fruits and flowers increases. The production capacity of the crop increases by 20 to 30 percent. This product can be used with any chemical and urea fertilizer.
