INTRODUCTION

What is Net House Farming?

Net house farming is a protected cultivation method in which crops are grown inside a structure covered with suitable agricultural nets. The structure helps create a more managed growing environment and can provide protection from selected external factors.

The right design depends on the crop, local climate, site conditions and the farmer’s objectives. A well-planned project starts with understanding these requirements before selecting the structure and materials.

Explore Different Types of Net Houses

Choose a suitable net house structure according to your crop and farming requirements.

01 / NET HOUSE TYPE

Shade Net House

Shade net houses use specially selected shade nets to regulate sunlight reaching the crops. They can support cultivation of plants that require partial shade and a managed growing environment.

Key Features

  • Helps manage sunlight exposure
  • Suitable shade net options
  • Supports nursery and plant cultivation
  • Structure can be planned according to crop needs
02 / NET HOUSE TYPE

Insect Proof Net House

Insect proof net houses use appropriate mesh nets around the structure to help reduce the entry of selected insects and pests into the growing area.

Key Features

  • Helps restrict selected insect entry
  • Mesh selection according to crop requirements
  • Supports protected vegetable cultivation
  • Requires suitable ventilation planning
03 / NET HOUSE TYPE

Naturally Ventilated Net House

Naturally ventilated structures are planned to encourage air movement through suitable openings and net coverings. Their design depends on local climate and crop requirements.

Key Features

  • Designed for natural air circulation
  • Suitable opening and net placement
  • Crop-specific structure planning
  • Can be planned for different growing needs
04 / NET HOUSE TYPE

Walk-in Tunnel Net House

Walk-in tunnel structures provide a covered growing space with an arched frame. They can be considered for selected crops and small-scale protected cultivation projects.

Key Features

  • Arched structural design
  • Covered crop growing space
  • Suitable for selected crop applications
  • Can be planned according to available land
