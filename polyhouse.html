What is Polyhouse Farming?

A polyhouse is a protected cultivation structure covered with transparent or translucent polyethylene film. It creates a growing environment where factors such as temperature, humidity, light and ventilation can be managed according to crop requirements.

Polyhouse farming helps growers plan cultivation more efficiently and protect crops from several external weather conditions. The structure and its features can be selected according to the local climate, available land and crop type.

01 / POLYHOUSE TYPE

Naturally Ventilated Polyhouse

Naturally ventilated polyhouses are designed with suitable side and roof openings that encourage natural air movement. This helps regulate internal temperature and humidity while providing a protected growing space.

Key Features

  • Natural air circulation through side and roof vents
  • Helps manage internal temperature and humidity
  • Reduced dependence on mechanical ventilation
02 / POLYHOUSE TYPE

Butterfly Ventilated Polyhouse

Butterfly ventilated polyhouses use specially designed roof vents that open like butterfly wings. This roof arrangement supports the release of warm air and improves natural ventilation inside the structure.

Key Features

  • Distinctive butterfly-style roof ventilation
  • Supports hot air escape and fresh air movement
  • Can help reduce the need for artificial cooling
03 / POLYHOUSE TYPE

Tropical Polyhouse

Tropical polyhouses are planned for warm and humid climatic conditions. Their design can incorporate suitable ventilation, cooling and irrigation arrangements to support crop growth in tropical environments.

Key Features

  • Designed with warm-climate conditions in mind
  • Ventilation and cooling options as required
  • Efficient irrigation planning for crop needs
04 / POLYHOUSE TYPE

Gothic Polyhouse

Gothic polyhouses feature a tall, pointed arch roof design. The structure offers useful internal height and supports air circulation, while its roof shape helps manage rainwater movement.

Key Features

  • Distinctive pointed arch roof design
  • Greater internal height and growing space
  • Designed for suitable structural strength and drainage
05 / POLYHOUSE TYPE

Customized Polyhouse

Customized polyhouses are planned around individual farm requirements. Design, dimensions, ventilation, covering materials and additional systems can be considered according to the site and crop requirements.

Key Features

  • Design planned according to farm requirements
  • Flexible structure size and layout options
  • Suitable systems selected for crop and climate needs
